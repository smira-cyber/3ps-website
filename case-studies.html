NOTE 01

Cloud migration without a single missed result

Situation. A healthcare organization's legacy systems were causing 40+ hours of downtime a month — every hour a queue of delayed results and frustrated clinicians.

Intervention. Staged migration to cloud infrastructure with parallel-run cutovers, so production never depended on a single big-bang weekend. Interfaces were moved last, monitored hop by hop.

Result. 50% IT cost reduction and 100% uptime for critical systems since cutover.

NOTE 02

200 daily tickets, automated down to a hum

Situation. A technology company's internal IT team was drowning in 200+ tickets a day — almost all of them repetitive, none of them strategic.

Intervention. Self-healing automation for the top recurring categories, intelligent triage for the rest, and escalation paths that put humans only where judgment is required.

Result. 30% reduction in IT workload, with the team redeployed to project work.

NOTE 03

Intervention. Zero-trust architecture, EDR with 24/7 SOC coverage, and a continuously maintained evidence trail mapped to the SOC 2 controls that failed.
