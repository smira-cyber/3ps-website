How we work

A procedure, not a pitch.

The Enterprise IT & Security Baseline Framework is the same structured sequence on every engagement. You always know which step you're on, what it produces, and what comes next.

STEP 01

Establish the baseline

Two weeks, structured. We inventory every system, interface, credential boundary, and dependency; identify who actually owns what; and write it down. No remediation yet — just a defensible snapshot of current reality that both sides agree is true.

  • Full asset, interface, and dependency inventory
  • Security and compliance posture, evidenced
  • OUT: a written report you own, whoever you hire
STEP 02

Align risk & priorities

Technical findings translated into business terms — what could stop the operation, what could fail an audit, what merely costs money. You set the risk tolerance; we sequence the work against it.

  • Findings ranked by operational impact, not vendor hype
  • OUT: a sequenced plan with owners and dates
STEP 03

Stabilize & govern

Exposure comes down first: backup integrity, endpoint coverage, access control, the obvious doors. Then governance goes in — change control, documentation standards, escalation paths — so improvements stick.

  • Change control that respects validated environments
  • OUT: stable operations under documented control
STEP 04

Operate with visibility

The steady state: 24/7 monitoring, the daily ops report, monthly service reviews, and quarterly executive briefings with audit-ready evidence. Boring, on purpose, indefinitely.

  • OUT: decisions you can defend to a board or auditor
