01 The problem

Your EDR can't stop what it can't see coming.

You've invested in CrowdStrike, SentinelOne, Microsoft Defender — state-of-the-art EDR detecting threats at machine speed. But attackers have adapted. Modern ransomware doesn't announce itself: it masquerades as legitimate processes and weaponizes your own trusted tools — PowerShell, WMI, RDP, SMB.

Your EDR tells you what happened. It doesn't prevent execution, close unnecessary network pathways, or ringfence trusted applications. You need a layer that stops code from running in the first place.
